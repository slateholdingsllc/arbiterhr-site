## SECTION 1 — Content Disclaimer additions for portal launch

Add to arbiter-content-disclaimer.md after the "How to Use Arbiter HR Workbooks" section:

### Use of the Arbiter HR Portal

Subscribers to the Arbiter HR portal access additional tools beyond the workbooks: HR bots scoped to specific topics, a monthly compliance digest dashboard, an audit-readiness archive, the LOA intake agent, and a demographics heatmap showing the geographic distribution of your workforce headcount.

**HR bots.** The portal includes bots that respond to user inputs by surfacing relevant rules, citations, and considerations. The bots are scoped to specific topic areas and are designed to support your research, not replace your judgment. Bot outputs are calculations and information, not recommendations, and not advice.

**LOA intake agent.** The LOA intake agent generates leave timelines (Gantt charts) and benefits snapshots based on the inputs you provide (state, employer benefits offered, pay rate, and leave situation). These outputs represent a calculation of how the cited leave statutes interact given your inputs. They are not a recommendation that you grant a specific leave, deny a specific leave, or take any specific action regarding any specific employee. Specific employee situations frequently involve facts (accommodation requests, interactive process obligations, performance issues, return-to-work questions) that the agent is not designed to address and that require counsel.

**Audit-readiness archive.** The portal's audit archive documents your engagement with Arbiter HR reference materials over time. It demonstrates that you accessed, reviewed, or generated compliance-related content as part of your HR workflow. It does not certify that any specific compliance action you took was legally correct. In an actual audit, investigation, or litigation, the archive evidences your monitoring activity; the compliance work itself, and whether it was legally sufficient, remains your responsibility.

**Demographics heatmap.** The heatmap displays the geographic distribution of headcount you input. It is a visualization tool. It does not assess your compliance posture, your workforce composition, or your employment-law exposure in any jurisdiction.

### Add new sections to the Full Version after Section 1 (Workbooks):

### 1A. Portal Subscription — 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

The Arbiter HR portal is sold as a monthly subscription through Stripe. We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on your first paid month.

**The 7-day window** begins on the date of your first successful payment and ends 7 calendar days later at 11:59 PM Pacific time on the seventh day.

**How to request:** email hello@arbiterhr.com within the 7-day window with your account email and a brief description of why you're requesting the refund. You don't need to provide an elaborate justification, but a sentence or two helps us understand whether something is broken on our end and worth fixing.

**What we'll do:** refund requests submitted within the 7-day window will be reviewed within 5 business days. If approved, we will issue a full refund of the amount paid through Stripe. Refunds typically post within 5-10 business days depending on your bank.

**When access ends:** when a refund is issued, portal access ends immediately upon refund processing; you do not retain access through the end of the monthly period.

Refunds are at our discretion. The vast majority of legitimate requests within the 7-day window will be honored. We reserve the right to decline refund requests that show patterns of abuse — for example, repeated subscribe-then-refund cycles from the same organization, requests that follow extensive use of the LOA intake agent or other bot tools, or requests that appear to be timed to extract value before the refund window closes. In evaluating abuse patterns, we may consider factors including portal usage volume, time elapsed since subscription, and prior account history.

### 1B. Portal — After 7 Days (Cancellation Without Refund)

Refunds are not offered for portal subscriptions cancelled after the 7-day window. This is true regardless of how much of the current monthly period remains.

If you cancel mid-month, you will retain portal access through the end of your paid month, but no partial refund will be issued for unused days. Monthly renewals do not generate a new refund window, and we do not issue refunds for monthly renewal charges after the renewal date.

### 1C. Portal — How to Cancel

You may cancel your portal subscription at any time through the Stripe Customer Portal accessible from your account settings, or by emailing hello@arbiterhr.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current monthly period. You retain portal access through that date, and no further charges are made.

There is no penalty for cancellation. You do not need to provide a reason. You may re-subscribe later at the then-current pricing.

### 7.5 Portal-specific content disclaimers

The portal includes interactive tools — HR bots, the LOA intake agent, monthly digests, audit archive, and heatmap — that generate outputs based on your inputs. These outputs are subject to the same disclaimers as workbook content:

- **Portal outputs are not legal advice.** Bot responses, LOA calculations, digest summaries, and audit archive entries are reference materials and calculations based on the inputs you provide. They are not legal advice for your specific situation.
- **The LOA intake agent generates calculations, not recommendations.** You provide structured inputs (state, employer benefits offered, pay rate, leave situation); the agent calculates how applicable leave statutes interact and produces a timeline visualization and a benefits snapshot. The output is a calculation of statutory entitlement based on your inputs. It is not a recommendation that you grant, deny, or otherwise handle any specific employee's leave in a particular way.
- **The audit archive documents engagement, not compliance.** Your archive shows that you accessed, reviewed, or generated specific portal content at specific times. It does not certify that your compliance actions were legally correct.
- **Specific situations require counsel.** Bot outputs, LOA calculations, and other portal content are not designed to address specific employee situations involving accommodation, interactive process, performance issues during leave, return-to-work questions, termination overlap, or any similar fact-specific scenario. These situations require attorney consultation.

## SECTION 5 — Operational notes for portal launch

When the portal launches, the following operational items need to align:

### Construction requirements for the LOA intake agent (from upl-safe-harbor-analysis.md)

Non-negotiable construction constraints — these are the difference between safe-harbor and exposure:

1. **Structured inputs only** — dropdowns, checkboxes, numeric fields. NOT free-text conversational interview.
2. **Outputs labeled as calculations** — "Leave Entitlement Calculation Based on Your Inputs," not "Recommended Leave Schedule."
3. **Disclaimer embedded in every export** — disclaimer travels with the Gantt chart or benefits snapshot when shared.
4. **"You should" language banned from agent outputs.**
5. **Out-of-scope refusal built in** — for accommodation, interactive process, performance issues during leave, return-to-work, termination overlap.

Operational discipline:

6. Specific-situation warnings that surface when inputs touch sensitive scenarios
7. Counsel-routing language built into deflection responses
8. Quarterly review of agent outputs for drift toward advice-like language

### Construction requirements for general bots

1. Scope discipline — each bot scoped to one topic area
2. Output framing — rules and citations, never "you should"
3. Specific-situation deflection — bot surfaces general rules, routes to counsel for specifics
4. No legal opinion language — no "in my opinion," "courts would likely rule"
5. Disclaimer surfacing in every bot session
6. Quarterly review for drift toward advice-like outputs
