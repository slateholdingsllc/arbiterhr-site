Common questions

Before you ask.

What's in the free compliance calendar?+
A one-page PDF mapping 40+ federal HR deadlines across 2026 — ACA, ERISA, Form 5500, 401(k), COBRA, W-2, 1099, EEO-1 — plus a printable cheat sheet of state pay-frequency rules and a quick reference card for new-hire reporting windows. Sent the moment you confirm your email. Get yours →
What's the difference between a single workbook, a bundle, and the catalog?+
A single workbook is one topic across all 50 states + DC for $39 — the right pick if you have one specific question. A bundle is four workbooks grouped around a workflow (payroll or generalist) for $129 — saves about $27 vs buying individually. The Complete Catalog is all 9 workbooks for $249 — saves $100+ and includes Predictive Scheduling. See them in the shop →
What does "updated monthly" actually mean?+
On the 1st of every month, every past buyer gets an email with the new edition attached — no re-purchase, no extra fee. You own the latest version forever. Refreshes are tracked against primary sources: DOL guidance, state labor departments, IRS publications.
What's the difference between the workbooks and the future portal?+
Workbooks are one-time purchases — they live in Excel and Google Sheets, work offline, and refresh monthly via email. A live portal is on the roadmap for later: daily-refreshed data scoped to your states and headcount, with a chatbot trained on the modules you own. The workbooks are the snapshot you can hold. The portal will be the live feed.
Is this legal advice?+
No. Arbiter HR products are reference materials — citations, summaries, and decision support for HR and payroll professionals. They're not a substitute for legal counsel on specific situations. Every workbook says this on tab 1.
What happens to my email when I download the calendar?+
It's used to deliver the calendar PDF, then to send four short emails with state-by-state HR insights over the following weeks. After that, the only thing that hits your inbox is the monthly edition email if you become a customer. No third-party sharing. Unsubscribe link on every email.
